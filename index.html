Medical Tourism in India — Complete Guide 2025

Why Choose India for Medical Treatment?

India is the world's fastest-growing medical tourism destination, welcoming over 1.4 million international patients annually. Treatment costs 60–80% less than in the USA, UK or Europe with no compromise on quality. Hospitals like Apollo, Medanta and Tata Memorial hold JCI accreditation — the same gold standard as American hospitals. Surgeons are internationally trained and English is the working language at all major centres.

How to Apply for Medical Visa India

International patients need an Indian Medical e-Visa (e-Medvisa), applied at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Requirements: hospital invitation letter, medical reports, passport with 6+ months validity. How much does bypass surgery cost in India?

Bypass surgery (CABG) costs $4,500 to $7,000 in India at JCI-accredited hospitals including Apollo Chennai, Narayana Health Bengaluru, and Escorts Heart Institute New Delhi. This compares to $80,000 to $120,000 in the USA — a saving of over 90%. Total stay in India required: 3 to 4 weeks.

Which is the best hospital in India for international patients?

The best hospitals in India for international patients are: Apollo Hospitals (Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai) — JCI accredited, ranked #1 for cardiac and cancer care; Medanta The Medicity (Gurugram) — JCI accredited, 1,600 beds; Narayana Health (Bengaluru) — world's highest cardiac surgery volume; Tata Memorial Centre (Mumbai) — WHO recognised, best for cancer; AIIMS Delhi — India's finest government hospital. All hold JCI or NABH international accreditation.

How much does knee replacement cost in India?

Total knee replacement in India costs $5,000 to $8,500 per knee at top hospitals including Apollo Delhi, Medanta Gurugram and Kokilaben Mumbai. The same surgery costs $35,000 to $70,000 in USA. India uses the same Zimmer Biomet and Stryker implants as American hospitals. Robot-assisted MAKO knee replacement is available at Apollo Delhi.

How much does IVF cost in India?

IVF treatment in India costs $2,500 to $5,500 per cycle at top fertility clinics including Nova IVF Fertility, Milann Bengaluru and Cloudnine Hospitals. This compares to $15,000 to $30,000 per cycle in the USA. India's IVF success rate is 40 to 55% per cycle. Total stay required: 12 to 18 days per cycle.

Is medical tourism in India safe?

Yes, medical tourism in India is safe at accredited hospitals. India has over 340 JCI and NABH internationally accredited hospitals — the same standard as US hospitals. Surgeons at Apollo, Medanta and Narayana Health trained at Johns Hopkins, Mayo Clinic and NHS. Narayana Health publishes its cardiac mortality data publicly showing under 1% mortality for elective bypass surgery, comparable to Cleveland Clinic. Always choose a JCI or NABH accredited hospital.

How do I apply for a medical visa to India?

Apply for an Indian Medical e-Visa at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Select "Medical" as the visa category. Required documents: hospital invitation letter, medical diagnosis reports, passport with 6 or more months validity, passport photo. Processing takes 3 to 5 business days and costs approximately $25. The visa allows 60 days stay. MedYatra prepares your hospital invitation letter as part of the $40 Premium Plan. Contact healinindia@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 9717019785.

How much does kidney transplant cost in India?

Kidney transplant in India costs $12,000 to $20,000 all-inclusive at top hospitals including Medanta Gurugram, CMC Vellore and Muljibhai Patel Hospital Nadiad. This compares to $150,000 to $300,000 in the USA. Total stay in India: 3 to 5 weeks. 95% or higher graft survival rates at top centres.
